In firmware 1.2 the [`DigitalOutputSet`](xref:Harp.LoadCells.DigitalOutputSet), [`DigitalOutputClear`](xref:Harp.LoadCells.DigitalOutputClear), [`DigitalOutputToggle`](xref:Harp.LoadCells.DigitalOutputToggle), and [`DigitalOutputState`](xref:Harp.LoadCells.DigitalOutputState) registers treat bit 0 as **DO0** and bits 1 to 8 as **DO1** to **DO8**, while the `DigitalOutputs` mask in `device.yml`, and therefore the Bonsai interface, assigns bit 0 to `DO1`. Until this is reconciled, selecting `DO1` in Bonsai may drive the **DO0** BNC, every other output may be shifted down by one, **DO8** may be unreachable, and a threshold configured for **DO1** may fire **DO0**. The examples in this guide follow the `device.yml` names.
